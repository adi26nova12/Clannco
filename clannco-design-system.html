01 · Color

Forest green is the brand. Brass is the signal — use it for CTAs, key data, and accents only. Cream is the canvas. If a page is more than ~10% brass, it's wrong.

02 · Typography

Three families, three jobs. Barlow Condensed for headlines — always uppercase, always heavy. Barlow for everything readable. JetBrains Mono for labels, data, and anything technical.

Display / H1 — Barlow Condensed 700 · 48–72px · uppercase · line-height 1.05
Scale Without Scaling Headcount
H2 — Barlow Condensed 600 · 32–40px · uppercase
Automation That Pays For Itself
H3 — Barlow Condensed 600 · 22–26px · uppercase
Weekly Intelligence Dockets
Body — Barlow 400 · 15–17px · line-height 1.6
We build business operating systems that eliminate manual work, improve decision making, and increase revenue. Every system is designed around one question: what would remove this bottleneck forever?
Small / Caption — Barlow 400 · 13px · Ink Soft
Estimates based on modeled data. Verified figures are marked in every report.
Mono / Label — JetBrains Mono 500 · 11–13px · letter-spacing .1em · uppercase for labels
PIPELINE HYGIENE SCAN · RUN #142 · 07 JUL 2026

Type Rules

Do
  • Uppercase all Barlow Condensed headings
  • Use mono eyebrows above headings (brass, letterspaced)
  • Keep body copy at 15–17px, max ~65ch line length
  • Use mono for numbers in tables and stats
Don't
  • Use Barlow Condensed for body text
  • Mix a fourth typeface — ever
  • Set headings in sentence case
  • Use italics (not in the system)

05 · Voice

Copy is part of the design system. Clannco speaks like an experienced operator, not a startup.

Do
  • Lead with outcomes: hours saved, leads captured, revenue protected
  • Be specific: "nightly hygiene scan", not "smart automation"
  • State uncertainty plainly — confidence tiers exist for a reason
  • Short sentences. Concrete verbs.
Don't
  • "Revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "game-changing"
  • Sell technology instead of outcomes
  • Imply client results that don't exist — capability proof only until signed
  • Exclamation marks
